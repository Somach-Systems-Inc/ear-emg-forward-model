# Licensing

| What | License | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Code | **Apache-2.0** | Includes an explicit patent grant and retaliation clause. Given the silent-speech patent landscape (US10878818 et al.), that protection is worth more than MIT's brevity. |
| Data, figures, model outputs | **CC-BY-4.0** | Standard for research artifacts; requires attribution. |
| Paper text | **CC-BY-4.0** | arXiv-compatible. |

Add the actual `LICENSE` file (Apache-2.0 full text) before making the repo public — `gh repo create` can add it, or copy from apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.txt

**One thing to decide deliberately:** your stated moat is "cheapest open stack that works," which argues for opening everything. But the electrode-positioning jig geometry and any montage you discover to be unusually good are the parts a competitor could lift directly. Consider keeping jig CAD in a private repo until you've decided whether it's a paper figure or a product feature.
